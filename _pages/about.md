I am an Innocore Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Vision and Learning Lab](https://vision.unist.ac.kr/intro.html) at [Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST)](https://www.unist.ac.kr/unist/index.do), South Korea, under the supervision of [Prof. Sengryul Baek](https://sites.google.com/site/bsrvision00/home). I completed my Ph.D. at the [Machine Learning and Visual Computing Lab](https://mlvc.khu.ac.kr/) at [Kyung Hee University](https://www.khu.ac.kr/eng/user/main/view.do), where I successfully defended my dissertation in June 2025. During my doctoral studies, I was also affiliated with [Télécom Paris](https://enzotarta.github.io/), where I spent seven months as a Ph.D. intern.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) in 2018, graduating with distinction (Gold Medal). I had the privilege of working under the supervision of [Prof. Sung Ho Bae](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EULut5oAAAAJ&hl=en) and collaborating with [Prof. Hui Yong Kim](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qbiBc50AAAAJ&hl=en), [Prof. Chaoning Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lvhxhyQAAAAJ&hl=en), [Prof. Giuseppe Valensize](https://l2s.centralesupelec.fr/u/valenzise-giuseppe/), and [Prof. Enzo Tartaglione](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uKuvN64AAAAJ&hl=en).

My research focuses on learned image compression and 3D Gaussian Splatting compression, with an emphasis on efficient and scalable visual representations. I have authored over 20 publications, including papers in top-tier venues such as NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, TCSVT, BMVC, and WACV, as well as several SCIE-indexed journals.

Beyond research, I am an avid cricket enthusiast and a passionate student of history.
